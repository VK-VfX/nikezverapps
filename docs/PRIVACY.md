# Privacy

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of ours. Everything below stays on your phone unless it says otherwise.

**What leaves the phone, and why** (only to run the feature you asked for):
| App | Sent | To | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purrcast | your coordinates (and your IP address, as with any request) | Open-Meteo | forecast, air quality, place search |
| Purrcast | your coordinates | OpenStreetMap (Nominatim; Overpass only when the map names a council instead of a place) | the name of where you are |
| Maps and Cats | what you type in search; start and end of a route; the map area you view | Photon, Nominatim, OSRM, OpenFreeMap | search, routes, map tiles |
| Maps and Cats | a Wikipedia search for your words (when you use "Search the web") | Wikipedia | places with an article |
| all three | the app name and version (and nothing about you) | GitHub | update check, download |

**On the phone only:** your streaks, favourites, saved places, settings and the diagnostic log.

**Streak numbers** (count, best, last visit day) and Purrcast's weather mood can be read by the other two Nikezvera apps through a read-only link. Other apps on the phone could read them too. They contain no personal information.

**Diagnostic log:** About, "Log for bug reports". It stays on the phone. It holds starts, crashes, update steps and network errors, never places, searches or coordinates. Nothing is sent unless you tap Share and choose where it goes.

**Delete everything:** clear each app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall.
