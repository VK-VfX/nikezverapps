Generative pitch-black AMOLED wallpapers for Android 10+, and a Windows desktop app.

**8.0.0:** a redesign. A new **Night Photos** world with six photographs pushed to pure black (Milky Way, Aurora, Skyline, Moon, Neon, Jellyfish). The editor is now short tabs (Design, Color, Tune, Words, Save) so the wallpaper stays in view, and Detail and Black level are one tap away on every wallpaper. Softer, calmer buttons. Doodle and Lab wallpapers now mix big hero doodles with tiny filler marks, and Density controls how close they get, from airy to crumpled together. The "For Pixel" world is gone; favorites you saved from it still open. Your favorites and settings stay.
