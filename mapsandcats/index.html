                  How Quest and the Cat Club work

                  Cats caught. In Quest mode, tap a cat or walk past it to catch it. Every catch adds to your total, which is your Cat Club number.

                  XP and levels. A catch gives 10–90 XP depending on the cat (plain cats are common, a heart-eyes cat is the rarest). Walking gives 1 XP for every 25 m. Level = 1 + the square root of (XP ÷ 60), rounded down, so level 2 is at 60 XP, level 3 at 240, level 4 at 540.

                  The Cat Club. Purrcast counts day streaks and Zamoled counts wallpapers you keep. Each app shows the others' numbers when installed. Nothing is uploaded.

                  Rewards. Level-up cheers here. Every 10 days of your Purrcast streak also earns a special wallpaper in Zamoled.
